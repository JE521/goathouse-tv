Everything the house has, one remote away.

It's built on the excellent CleanYourTV, which safely replaces your TV's launcher and debloats it. Goathouse TV adds the media and smart-home layer.

Rows that know you

Continue Watching and Next Up for whoever's watching, plus Recently Added, playlists and new music.

Who's watching

Your Jellyfin users with their avatars, plus a Family mode. The rows and the music follow the person.

A real music app

Albums, artists, playlists and Up Next from Music Assistant or Navidrome, with A–Z jump, gapless playback and media keys.

A synced speaker

The TV joins Music Assistant as a Sendspin player and plays in time with every other room.

Your own voice assistant

The mic button runs your Home Assistant Assist pipeline. In-app mic buttons use your Whisper too.

Photos everywhere

Immich albums become the background and the screensaver.

Set up from your phone

Settings → Accounts shows a QR code and a PIN. Fill in your servers on your phone, and each one is tested live as you type.

Nothing is typed with the remote, and nothing secret ever goes in a theme, a script or the repo.

Install in an evening.

You need a PC with adb, JDK 17 and the Android SDK (platform 34), and network debugging switched on in the TV's Developer options. It's tested on onn 4K and onn FHD Google TV boxes.

Get the code and name your TVs

git clone https://github.com/JE521/goathouse-tv && cd goathouse-tv
cp goathouse/hosts.env.example goathouse/hosts.env   # living=192.168.1.50
python3 -m venv tools/venv && tools/venv/bin/pip install -r tools/requirements.txt

Build and install the launcher

The stock launcher is only disabled once the new one is confirmed as Home.

(cd launcher && ./gradlew assembleRelease)
INPUT_KEY_SERVICE=1 SKIP_BUILD=1 TV_HOST=192.168.1.50 launcher/build-and-install.sh
goathouse/push-theme.sh living

Connect your servers

On the TV, go to Settings → Accounts, scan the code, and fill in what you run.

Optional: debloat, reversibly

Google TV's own apps are disabled, never uninstalled, one batch at a time.

goathouse/debloat.sh living goathouse/debloat/1-telemetry.txt
TV_HOST=192.168.1.50 tools/restore.sh goathouse/debloat/1-telemetry.txt   # undo

Close ADB when you're done

Network debugging on port 5555 is plaintext. Switch it off and check with tools/adb-closed.py.

The full guide covers per-person Jellyfin, remote buttons, Obtainium and your own tile art: goathouse/README.md.

Private by design.

It's a template anyone can use, and none of your values ever have to touch git.

On the TV, not in the repo

Server addresses, API keys, passwords and tokens live in the launcher's private storage, entered from your phone.

Git-ignored by default

Your TVs' addresses (goathouse/hosts.env) and your own tile art (goathouse/art/) stay local.

No Google, no telemetry

No sign-ins and no analytics. The TV only talks to the servers you give it.
